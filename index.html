1. Information we do not collect

The App has no registration, no login, and no user profile. We do not collect, request, or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • your date of birth, time of birth, or place of birth;
  • your precise or approximate location — the App requests no location permission;
  • photographs, camera or microphone access, or any biometric information, including face or fingerprint data — the App requests no camera permission and contains no camera feature;
  • your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, calendar, or files on your device;
  • payment or financial information — the App contains no purchases.

The App's palm reading feature is a set of multiple-choice questions about the lines on your hand. It does not use your camera, and no photograph of your hand is taken, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone. It is not transmitted to us, and we cannot read it:

All of this is removed when you uninstall the App, or when you clear the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Horosigns → Storage.

3. Information sent to our servers

To show you a horoscope, the App asks our content server for the text that matches the zodiac sign you selected and the date being viewed. To show articles, it requests the article list and article content.

These requests contain the zodiac sign identifier, a date, and article identifiers. They contain no account, no device identifier, and nothing that identifies you personally.

As with any request made over the internet, our server receives the IP address the request came from, together with standard technical information such as the time of the request. We use this only to operate the service, diagnose faults, and protect against abuse. We do not use it to build a profile of you and we do not combine it with advertising identifiers.

4. Advertising and the advertising ID

The App is free and is supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob, provided by Google, to display banner and interstitial ads.

To serve, measure, and protect those ads, the Google AdMob software development kit collects the Android advertising ID (AAID) from your device, along with technical information such as your device model, operating system version, country, and interactions with the ads shown. This information is collected by Google and shared with Google as an independent recipient, for advertising and for fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Depending on your settings and the rules that apply in your country, ads may be personalized (chosen partly on the basis of your interests) or non-personalized (chosen without using your advertising ID for interest profiling).

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies, not by ours:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and privacy

How to control this

You can change or remove your advertising ID at any time, without uninstalling the App:

  • Reset the ID: Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete the ID entirely: the same screen offers Delete advertising ID on Android 12 and later. After this, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID and cannot use it to personalize ads.
  • Turn off ad personalization: the same screen offers Opt out of Ads Personalization. Ads will still appear, but will not be personalized.

Menu names differ slightly between Android versions and phone manufacturers.
